About Me

As a Software QA Manager and Test Lead, I focus on delivering reliable, high-quality software through effective testing strategies, structured quality assurance processes, and continuous improvement. My expertise includes test planning, test management, manual and automated testing, defect tracking, risk assessment, and cross-functional team collaboration. I work closely with developers, product owners, and stakeholders to identify issues early, improve testing efficiency, and ensure software meets business and user requirements. My goal is to strengthen software quality, reduce delivery risks, and support confident, successful product releases.
